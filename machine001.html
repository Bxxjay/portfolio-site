Websites are an essential part of our digital world. They are more than just pages on the internet — they are spaces where people share ideas, businesses connect with customers, and communities come together. A website can be simple, like a personal blog, or complex, like an online store with thousands of products, but no matter the size, it plays a crucial role in how we communicate and interact online.

At their core, websites are built using three main technologies: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. HTML provides the structure of the site, defining headings, paragraphs, images, and buttons. CSS brings the website to life by styling it — adding colors, fonts, spacing, and layouts that make the pages visually appealing. JavaScript adds behavior, allowing the website to respond to user actions, update content dynamically, and create interactive experiences.
